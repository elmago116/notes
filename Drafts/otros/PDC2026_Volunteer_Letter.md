**To:** PDC 2026 Organizing Committee  
**Email:** pdc2026-design@polimi.it  
**Subject:** Application to Volunteer at PDC 2026

Dear PDC 2026 Organizing Committee,

I am writing to express my sincere interest in volunteering for the Participatory Design Conference 2026, which will be held at the Politecnico di Milano Campus Bovisa Durando in Milan, Italy. As a first-year doctoral student who has been accepted to participate in the PhD Colloquium at this conference, I am eager to contribute to the success of this important gathering while deepening my engagement with the participatory design community.

Having received confirmation of my acceptance to the PhD Colloquium, I am particularly excited about the opportunity to engage with the conference proceedings and contribute to the scholarly activities that will shape this event. The conference theme of "Peace, Dialogue, Coexistence: Designing for living together" resonates deeply with my research interests, and I believe that active participation as a volunteer would enhance both my academic development and my understanding of participatory design methodologies.

I am especially interested in learning about the ACM TAPS (The ACM Article Processing System) workflow, as I understand that all conference contributions will be published open access in the ACM Digital Library. While I must acknowledge that I currently have no prior experience with the ACM TAPS workflow, I am confident that my engineering and humanistic background positions me well to learn and master these skills successfully. My technical training provides me with the analytical mindset and systematic approach necessary to understand complex workflows, while my humanistic education has developed my attention to detail, precision in language, and understanding of scholarly communication standards. I am highly motivated to acquire these skills and contribute to the publication process, and I am committed to dedicating the necessary time and effort to master the technical requirements of the system. My enthusiasm for academic publishing and my desire to support the open access dissemination of research make this an ideal opportunity for professional development.

I would be grateful for the opportunity to contribute to PDC 2026 while learning from experienced researchers and practitioners in the field. Volunteering would not only allow me to give back to the participatory design community but would also provide invaluable experience in conference organization and academic publishing workflows that will benefit my future career.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to the success of PDC 2026 and to learning more about ACM TAPS and conference organization processes. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require any additional information or clarification regarding my application.

Yours sincerely,
