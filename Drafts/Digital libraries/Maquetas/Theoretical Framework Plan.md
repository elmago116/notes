### 3. Evaluation Framework Structure (150-180 words)
Based on Table 1, four analytical dimensions:
1. User integration phases and process-driven patterns
   - Design vs research processes
   - Early, constant, late integration
   - References: Waidelich et al. (2018), Calvano (2025), Turnhout et al. (2020), Haklay et al. (2020), Fox & Chandrasekar (2021)

2. Participatory approaches and methodological tools
   - Co-design, user testing, crowdsourcing, annotation
   - References: Delgado et al. (2023), Stappers & Sanders (2008), Liu (2025), Gómez-Ferri (2014)

3. Levels of user participation and engagement
   - Consult, include, elaborate, own
   - Scientific, educational, social, political agency
   - References: Delgado et al. (2023), Vohland et al. (2021), Matt (2015)

4. Inclusion, representational gaps and epistemic justice
   - Gender, intersectionality, bias mitigation
   - Knowledge diversity, power dynamics
   - References: Fricker (2007), Haraway (1991), Stengers & Despret (2014), Escobar (2016)

### 4. Synthesis (50-70 words)
