3. **World Literature Knowledge Graph:** [[The World Literature Knowledge Graph|Stranisci et al., 2017]] - Integration of three reader communities into a shared semantic model; ongoing use and reception data (involve/collaborate).
4. **PhD Students' Information-Sharing Knowledge Graph:** [[User-driven efforts in creating knowledge graph information system|(Gardasevic & Gazan, 2020)]] [[Community design of a knowledge graph to support interdisciplinary PhD Students.pdf|(2023)]][[It answers questions that I didn't know I had" PhD students' evaluation of an information sharing knowledge graph|(2024)]]  - Participatory design workshop with PhD students plus mixed-methods evaluation (interviews and sentiment analysis); strong participatory design + HCI linkage (collaborate).
5. **Research Knowledge Graphs for Participatory Archives:** [[Metadata and Semantic Research - Conference.pdf|(Hocker et al., 2022)]] - Archivists and researchers customize and enrich school archives; long-term collaborative curation (collaborate/ongoing community curation).
6. **KnowEdu:** [[KnowEdu_ A System to Construct Knowledge Graph for Education – DOAJ.pdf|(Chen et al., 2018)]] - Explicit user-centred design involving experts and users; focus on iterative refinement of educational knowledge representations (consult/involve).
7. **Polariscope Collaborative Storytelling Platform:** [[Remixing and repurposing cultural heritage archives through a collaborative and AI-generated storytelling digital platform|(Almeida et al., 2024)]] - Collaborative storytelling platform to share, visualize and co-create collective memories over cultural heritage archives; gathers citizens and institutions, integrates AI-generated stories (GPT‑3.5) with preliminary user testing (involve/collaborate).
8. **GTDOnto:** [[GTDOnto- An Ontology for Organizing and Modeling Knowledge about Global Terrorism.pdf|(Al-Fayez et al., 2023)]] - NeOn methodology with UX aspects in a sensitive terrorism domain; expert-led design with attention to user tasks and potential bias issues (consult, bias-aware modelling).
9. **DigiNUMA:** [[Potentials of Research Knowledge Graphs for Interlinking Participatory Archives|(Hocker et al., 2022)]] - Explicit participatory heritage component; collaboration between heritage professionals and communities around numismatic and archaeological data (involve/collaborate).
10. **SAMPO Model Projects:** [[Digital humanities on the Semantic Web - Sampo model and portal series.pdf|(Hyvönen, 2023)]] [[Publishing and Studying Historical Opera and Music Theatre Performances on the Semantic Web- Case OperaSampo 1830–1960|(Ahola et al., 2024)]]; [[Analyzing the Lives of Finnish Academic People 1640-1899 in Nordic and Baltic Countries- AcademySampo Data Service and Portal|Leskinen et al., 2022)]] - User-oriented semantic portals emphasizing exploration and reuse; interaction via configurable views and faceted exploration, without formal participatory design (inform/involve through rich exploration tools).
11. **Wikibase for Cultural Heritage:** [[Using Wikibase for Managing Cultural Heritage Linked Open Data Based on CIDOC CRM|(Kesäniemi et al., 2022)]]- Collaborative editing environment where non-expert users can contribute; continuous crowdsourced enrichment and correction (community curation, ongoing collaboration).
12. **Cultural Leaf Semantic Portal:** [[Cultural Leaf - a LOD portal for exploring the cultural heritage|(Dorobăț, I.C. et al., 2024)]] - Usability-focused portal design; interaction framed through exploratory use and (implicitly) usability considerations, though evaluation processes are under-documented (inform/involve).
13. **European Cultural Gems:** [[Cultural gems linked open data- Mapping culture and intangible heritage in European cities|(Consoli et al., 2023)]] - Crowdsourced platform where citizens contribute information about cultural and creative places; ongoing citizen-contribution model with linked open data backend (crowdsourcing / citizen participation).
14. **ARCA:** [[Design, realization, and user evaluation of the ARCA system for exploring a digital library.pdf|(Bernasconi et al., 2022)]] - User-centred, iterative semantic search system; two releases with comparative usability testing position users as evaluators and co-shapers of functionality (HCI evaluation; involve/collaborate).
15. **InTaVia:** [[The Multiple Faces of Cultural Heritage Towards an Integrated Visualization Platform for Tangible and Intangible Cultural Assets|(Mayr et al., 2022)]] [[The InTaVia Knowledge Graph–European National Biographical and Cultural Heritage Object Data.pdf|(Schlögl et al., 2025)]] - Requirement workshops and user testing on the interface side, plus empirical validation; strong HCI orientation with structured user-centred design across design and evaluation phases (collaborate).
16. **ATLAS:** [[ATLAS- Towards a Knowledge Graph of International Scholarly Research on the Italian Digital Cultural Heritage|(Giacomini et al., 2025)]]- Supports participatory research on Italian digital cultural heritage; researchers and possibly practitioners co-construct and interrogate the knowledge graph (collaborate within expert communities).
17. **Neurosymbolic Narrative Generation for Cultural Heritage:** [[Neurosymbolic Narrative Generation for Cultural Heritage.pdf|(Palma, 2023)]] - Emphasizes user empowerment in narrative generation; users move from consumers to co-authors by controlling narrative parameters (empower, HCI and participatory design blend).
18. **ArsEmotica:** [[ArsEmotica for arsmeteo.org- Emotion-driven exploration of online art collections|(Patti, V. et al., 2015)]] - Uses social media comments as user-generated emotional metadata; large-scale, implicit crowdsourcing of affective responses to art (crowdsourcing / community contribution).
19. **Participatory Indexing for Academic Digital Libraries:** [[Participatory Indexing in the Eyes of Its Potential Users- An Example of a Co-design of Participatory Services in an Academic Digital Library.pdf|(Leblanc, 2020)]] - Co-design workshops with potential users to prototype participatory indexing services; strong participatory design in shaping indexing workflows (collaborate/empower around metadata).
20. **ProvKOS:** [[A conceptual model for tracking the provenance of activities in knowledge organization systems.pdf|(Choi & Cheng, 2024)]] - Five-step participatory process engaging editorial managers, information professionals, and researchers to define provenance tracking; interaction focuses on negotiating controversial classifications and bias mitigation (collaborate).
21. **Inuvialuit Digital Library:** [[Community-Driven Knowledge Organization for Cultural Heritage Digital Libraries The Case of the Inuvialuit Settlement Region|(Farnel & Shiri, 2019)]] - Community-led co-design, digital storytelling workshops, and contributor-controlled narratives; strong community agency and culturally grounded access rules (collaborate/empower; epistemic justice-centred).
22. **HyperReal:** [[Multivocal Exhibition Exploring Cultural Perspectives through User-Curated Art Exhibitions|(Sartini, 2025)]] - Explicit participatory design of multivocal exhibitions; users act as co-curators, shaping narrative and interpretive layers over time (collaborate/empower).
23. **Rhizomer:** [[Rhizomer- Interactive semantic knowledge graphs exploration|(García et al., 2022)]] - Interactive tool for exploring knowledge graphs available as Semantic Web data; supports overview, zoom and filter, and details-on-demand tasks without requiring prior knowledge of dataset structure or SPARQL; user-centered interface design for researchers, practitioners, and lay users (inform/involve through exploration tools).
