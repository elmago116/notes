## APA 7th Edition Guidelines Reference

According to the [APA Style Guidelines](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines), complete citations require:

### Required Elements:
- **Author(s):** Last name, first initial(s)
- **Year:** Publication year in parentheses
- **Title:** Article or book title
- **Identifier:** DOI, URL, or ISSN for electronic sources

### Recommended Elements:
- **Journal name:** For journal articles
- **Volume/Issue:** For journal articles
- **Page numbers:** For journal articles and book chapters
- **Publisher:** For books and reports
- **Document type:** Article, book, conference proceeding, etc.

### Example APA 7th Edition Format:
```
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), Pages. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
```
