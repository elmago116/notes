*Information Systems Journal* is an international information technology journal publishing a broad range of impactful information systems (IS) research. ISJ publishes papers addressing the challenges and opportunities in making information systems a positive contribution to practice, policy, and society more broadly. We welcome information systems research that investigates urgent organizational, management and social phenomena.

## Information Systems Journal rated 4 in the 2024 CABS Academic Journal Guide

The purpose of the Academic Journal Guide is to assist researchers to make informed judgements about the outlets they may wish to publish in. It provides details on a wide range of journals, stretching across fields that are either central or salient to business and management studies.

The Academic Journal Guides’ ratings are based upon peer review, editorial and expert judgements.
