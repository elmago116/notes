## Biographies

Sara Beckman is on the faculty at the Haas School of Business at UC Berkeley, where she teaches new product development and design thinking. < [beckman@haas.berkeley.edu](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.2307/) >

Michael Barry is an Adjunct Professor in Stanford University's Department of Mechanical Engineering and founder of Point Forward, a consultancy that delivers innovation strategies based on customer insights. < [mbarry@pointforward.com](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.2307/) >
